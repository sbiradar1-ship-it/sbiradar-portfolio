Enterprise RAG search platform, 0-to-1

SAP · Principal PM

Legacy keyword search wasn't surfacing the right procurement content across Marketplace, Sourcing, Catalog, and Supplier Management. Led the architecture and launch of a semantic search platform built on NER, vector embeddings, and ML re-ranking.

Search workflow

01
Query intentNER + intent modeling
02
Hybrid retrievalLexical + vector, in parallel
03
MergeCombine candidate pools
04
ML re-rankingSentence-BERT re-rank
05
Serve <500msCloud-scale serving

Incoming queries are parsed with NER-based intent modeling to separate brand, product type, and spec constraints from free text. Retrieval then fans out in parallel across two arms — a lexical BM25 pass and a vector KNN pass over semantic embeddings — and both result sets are merged into a single candidate pool before a Sentence-BERT cross-encoder re-ranks them for relevance, all served in under 500ms at scale. RAGAS-based evaluation (faithfulness, context precision/recall) validated every iteration before rollout. Net effect: an 82% NDCG relevance lift and latency cut from 2s to 500ms, at 50M+ queries/day.

A catalog quality scoring system (rule-based checks plus an XGBoost model) flags low-quality items; catalog admins then use Perplexity AI to generate improved titles, descriptions, specs, and image suggestions, which are surfaced back to catalog providers. In parallel, a BERT-based NER pipeline extracts structured attributes (brand, product type, specs) feeding both enrichment and search, a keyword-to-category classifier flags low-confidence classifications for admin correction, and an LLM-driven compound-word system expands and normalizes search vocabulary for long-tail and multilingual recall. Net effect: ~40% less manual enrichment effort and materially better catalog completeness and consistency.
